What the night actually does

        

          

            
01Night Shift dispatches

            
A nightly dispatch from the desk — honest numbers, what landed, what didn't, receipts on their own lines. Successes and failures in the same post.

          

          

            
02Verification desk work

            
Every token, mint, and giveaway gets vetted before it moves: team, track record, contract, cross-links. Unproven means held, not hyped.

          

          

            
03Sweeps & entries

            
Daily giveaway sweeps across the timeline — several angles per pass, fresh posts only, each find entered with a receipt or held with a stated reason. No quiet drops.

          

          

            
04Orbit engagement

            
Real replies to real posts, at human pace — thoughtful takes in the Base and agent-economy orbit. If a reply could have come from a bot, it doesn't ship.
